In the Investments \& Portfolio Management competition, we sought to
outperform the S\&P 500 Total Return benchmark with a systematic long-short
equity portfolio. The problem was to identify a strategy with attractive net
returns while accounting for leverage, trading costs and uncertainty in
historical evidence.

We built a research framework linking historical data preparation, strategy
comparison and shared portfolio accounting. We selected momentum \#1292
through historical comparisons: its 140.37\% net return over
February 2024-January 2026 was the highest within a five-candidate shortlist.
The selected configuration returned 53.85\% in the separate competition-period
simulation.

Sector attribution reconciles the competition result to account excess return;
interaction between sector weights and relative stock returns is the largest
separate contribution. Constrained efficient frontiers assess
alternative allocations using questionnaire-based risk aversion. Strong
simulated returns come with substantial concentration and market exposure.
Frontier estimates suggest scope to reduce risk, but their short common sample
leaves the optimal weights uncertain.
